# Spec - tb-implementation-efficiency

**Feature:** `tb-implementation-efficiency`
**Created:** 2026-11-09
**Design:** [design.md](design.md), approved 2026-11-09
**Status:** Proposed delivery requirements derived from approved design; implementation not authorized.

## Summary

GateReeve supports agent-selected focused implementation checks, one consolidated fresh independent acceptance assessment, and a smaller evidence contract without recursive runtime fingerprinting. Per-workflow SQLite provides authoritative structured history; a separate shared metrics store supports reproducible fleet reports, drill-downs, and explicitly requested evidence-cited analysis through common CLI/MCP/desktop services.

## Definition of Done

The governing software-development workflow applies to delivery of this change. Its currently installed obligations remain in force until the changed product is approved, validated and activated. Passing document lint is not proof of implementation or independent review. Merge, publishing, installation and deployment retain their separate authorization requirements.

## Acceptance Criteria

- **AC1. Agent-selected validation and consolidated independent assessment.** Within authorized implementation, GateReeve permits the agent to select, order, batch, parallelize and repeat checks and reviews without a mandatory per-edit gate, fixed test ratio, fixed reviewer roster or separate mandatory Judge execution. Acceptance requires a fresh independent assessment explicitly addressing code defects/interactions, each applicable approved criterion, evidence sufficiency/limitations, material scope/decision contradictions and relevant findings/corrections. Implementer-context assessments, absent required conclusions, unsupported success claims and unresolved blocking findings do not qualify. An extra Judge-style or model-diverse assessment remains available at agent discretion. Canonical guidance communicates the test pyramid, early focused feedback and contextual encouragement of model diversity without deterministic reviewer triggers. Existing named obligations are accounted for by the new responsibility mapping rather than silently discarded. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge product behavior and package tests.

- **AC2. Evidence qualification without recursive runtime fingerprints.** New-contract execution, status, acceptance and protected delivery paths do not enumerate/hash installed dependency or external runtime trees for environment qualification, and require no per-file fingerprint cache or runtime-tree manifest. Checks retain actual source/configuration/scope, execution identity, observed result and available environment metadata. Git/document/artifact identities remain available for source provenance, integrity and approvals. Acceptance requires the applicable configured CI evidence for the actual candidate, with executed/skipped/failed/missing scope visible; an explicitly documented alternative when the CI backstop is absent or insufficient must be independently assessed. Old-source or late results are retained under their actual identity and cannot be promoted to new-candidate success. A past check is never represented as freshly executed. Local environment equivalence is not claimed without evidence. Explicit human authority and permitted source/target branch direction remain enforced. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge controlled executions, hosted-check fixtures and protection tests; real protected delivery is post-merge/release as separately authorized.

- **AC3. Isolated authoritative workflow memory.** A new issue/topic-branch/worktree workflow uses one local untracked SQLite database as the authoritative structured history, independent of chat-thread replacement and isolated from unrelated worktrees. Histories expose workflow/participant identity, executions/observations, findings/dispositions, correction links, assessments/coverage, actual human decisions, friction/outcome notes and evidence references. Facts, judgments, hypotheses, unknowns and superseded records remain distinguishable. Stable IDs and concise labels allow provenance without depending solely on absolute paths. Supported operations preserve committed records under concurrent local updates and prevent duplicate retries or silent replacement of another result/decision; long-running checks/reviews do not hold transactions open. No independently authoritative mirror journal, global workflow database or transcript archive is required. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge fresh-workflow, process-concurrency, restart and isolation tests against disposable databases.

- **AC4. Observable, nonblocking measurement.** Shared measurement records relevant protocol/setup/execution/review durations, parent relationships, retry/rerun reasons, strategy/model/version context and supported links from findings through fixes to later validation/outcomes. Collection states whether timing is observed, imported or incomplete; unobserved intervals, missing identities and unknown defect origin are not silently filled in. A metrics projection failure is reported without losing authoritative workflow results or granting passage. Projection retries do not duplicate observations. Default readable labels include project, issue, branch, check and model/provider when known; default exports omit raw command arguments, environment values and full logs. Large artifacts may remain referenced. Metrics retention never removes authoritative workflow records. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge collector/projection/failure fixtures plus observed timing on representative disposable workflows; no claim of complete fleet capture is required.

- **AC5. Fixed fleet reporting datasets.** Reporting snapshots from local work, Playpen, AWS and UpCloud can be exported and imported without live workflow-database synchronization or transfer of authority. Each import identifies its source, format/version, collection time, covered period and stable record provenance. Reimporting the same or overlapping observations does not double-count them; conflicting or unsupported input is surfaced rather than silently converted to valid history. A report identifies its fixed dataset, included/excluded platforms, stale or missing sources and historical mapping gaps. Reopening a report retains its dataset rather than silently querying newer source state. Supported historical rc.23/rc.24 metrics can contribute labeled observations without manufacturing missing names, timing or finding details. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge fixture/snapshot imports, including supplied historical exports and repeated/overlapping/missing-source cases; unavailable live hosts are disclosed, not prerequisites for software acceptance.

- **AC6. Useful deterministic reevaluation and drill-downs.** From a selected dataset, the report offers a concise all-project overview with project/workflow drill-downs and relevant check/reviewer/platform dimensions. It explains observed elapsed time, aggregate effort, setup/waits where observed, repeated validation/review and reasons, findings/corrections and linked escaped defects where known. It identifies workflow/policy/model context, sample sizes and coverage limits. Overlapping/nested operations and retries have defined, tested accounting; elapsed time is not reported as CPU time or billed cost, missing capture is not zero, and repeated checks/finding counts do not alone establish waste or quality. Missing source detail remains visible at drill-down. Equivalent datasets/filters yield equivalent structured results through shared interfaces. The factual report needs no LLM call and supports structured export and a readable HTML export. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge deterministic accounting fixtures, historical-data reconciliation and report-rendering tests.

- **AC7. Shared interfaces and bounded optional analysis.** CLI, MCP and desktop expose the same shared report query/import/export behavior and database visibility for known/explicit stores: location/purpose, issue/worktree association where applicable, schema/size/health, recent state and reporting-source coverage. Inspection does not silently migrate, prune or recursively scan unrelated directories. Explicitly requested agent analysis uses the fixed report dataset, cites supporting records, labels hypotheses and uncertainty, and proposes changes without applying them; factual reports remain available if analysis is unavailable or fails. Provider/model selection uses existing configured capabilities and errors remain explicit. Lightweight existing metrics cleanup is reused; workflow reset is offered only if safe and straightforward for an explicitly selected inactive workflow, preserving source/documents and not implying recovered authority. Unsupported/active reset is reported without cascading deletion. No general SQL editor, fleet synchronization or complex recovery subsystem is introduced. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge shared-service, CLI, MCP and desktop integration/rendered checks; actual installed interface smoke after separately authorized release/install.

- **AC8. Predictable rollout and simple loss handling.** New workflows use the new contract; existing sessions, documents and historical runtime files are neither automatically converted nor deleted. Explicit resume carries forward unchanged planning only with observed authority, identifies unavailable historical proof and obtains new-contract evidence without replaying old events into approval. A missing SQLite database can produce a visible fresh-run/restart path rather than fabricated history; surviving source/documents remain intact. Metrics imports cannot grant workflow authority. Lossless reconstruction, automatic backup, active workflow/T3-session migration, exhaustive transcript collection, scheduled reevaluation and autonomous policy changes are outside this release. Release/install smoke must distinguish shipped artifacts, installation and actual fresh-session activation. **Evidence boundary:** pre-merge byte-preservation, missing-database, resume and isolation fixtures; packaged installation/activation checks remain explicit post-merge obligations requiring separate authorization.

## Boundary and interpretation notes

- Pre-merge tests may use controlled provider/check/CI fixtures to demonstrate product semantics. They must be labeled as fixtures and cannot be presented as real feature reviews or hosted acceptance results.
- Product support for fleet snapshots does not require every machine online at acceptance. The report must accurately disclose unavailable data; fresh fleet baseline gathering remains a read-only investigation task.
- No numerical speedup or capture-percentage threshold is invented. Record baseline/candidate timings and coverage honestly; removal of traversal and redundant mandatory execution is directly verifiable behavior. Quality equivalence of consolidated review is a monitored hypothesis, not a promised fact.
- The optional minimal reset can be explicitly unavailable where safe coordination would become complex. This is the approved bounded management scope, not permission for bulk deletion.
- Local source inspection shows both GateReeve and MyLifeMap CI select suites conditionally. The implementation must distinguish requested, executed and skipped coverage and never assume every green workflow ran all suites.
